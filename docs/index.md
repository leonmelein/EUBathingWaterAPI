# 🇪🇺 EU Bathing Water API

## Mission statement
Bathing water quality in Europe is regulated by [EU Directive 2006/7/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02006L0007-20140101). This directive sets out how states need to monitor the quality of their bathing waters.Beyond the EU member states Albania and Switzerland also follow this directive. The United Kingdom was subject to these rules untill Brexit. The directive was retained unchanged in UK law. As such, their locations and test results are included.

Currently, current testing results and classifications are only visible on individual country, region or municipality websites. The European Environmental Agency does compile Europe-wide data on bathing water quality, but only does so after the fact.

I want to gather this data in real-time so swimmers and travelers across Europe can make informed decisions about where to swim safely. By providing insight into testing results and their historical trends, I want to increase people's awareness of the Bathing Water directive and advocacy for their favorite swimming spots.

## Guiding principles

- Get data directly from official government sources
- Gather list of locations for each country and combine them into an Europe wide overview
- Heavily prefer open data sources like WFS services or JSON feeds over more crude tools like scraping
