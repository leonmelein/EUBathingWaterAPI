About us

Where can you swim safely in Europe? This website contains all monitored bathing sites in the European Union and select other European countries who apply the European Bathing Wather Directive.

Measing bathing water quality

Directive 2006/7/EC sets out a comprehrensive system for appointing and monitoring open water bathing sites.
